How the model is trained

Each teacher is the best at one job. The student encoder learns from all three, with the 64M Zipformer-M as a teaching assistant, then gets fine-tuned on labelled data for every task.

Stage 1 / Distil

WavLM-Large, SSLAM and ReDimNet2-B6 act as teachers, and the finished 64M Zipformer-M acts as a frozen teaching assistant. The 22M student starts from three layer stacks copied from Zipformer-M (S1, S2, S6) and learns to reproduce what the teachers and the assistant know, without any labels.

Stage 2 / Fine-tune

The shared encoder and its three task heads train on LibriSpeech, FSD50K and VoxCeleb1 together. The teaching assistant stays in the loop: the student also learns to match Zipformer-M’s transcription, tagging and speaker outputs.

The teacher models

Each teacher is built for one task. The original MT2KD paper used Whisper-large-v3, BEATs and ECAPA-TDNN. We replaced each one after a measured comparison on our own data.

WavLM-Large

Microsoft · 2021

WavLM learns speech without transcripts. During pre-training it hears audio with parts masked out and other speech or noise mixed in, and learns to predict the clean speech underneath. MT2KD distils its final-layer features into the student for spoken content.

Parameters316M
ArchitectureCNN encoder + 24-layer Transformer
InputRaw 16 kHz waveform
Pre-training94k h: Libri-Light, GigaSpeech, VoxPopuli

Why WavLM: replaces Whisper-large-v3 from the paper. With the same student and 2 h of distillation, the WavLM-taught student reached 64.93% probe WER on dev-clean, against 81.93% for the Whisper-taught one.

SSLAM

Alex et al. · ICLR 2025

SSLAM is a self-supervised audio model that is also trained on mixtures of sounds, so it copes with recordings where several things happen at once. The fine-tuned version scores the 527 AudioSet classes. MT2KD distils those scores into the student.

Parameters90.4M
ArchitectureTransformer on spectrogram patches
Fine-tuned onAudioSet-2M (paper: 50.2 mAP)
Used hereAS2M fine-tuned checkpoint

Why SSLAM: replaces BEATs from the paper. On FSD50K validation (192 classes mapped by AudioSet ID) SSLAM scored 55.19% mAP, against 50.29% for BEATs.

ReDimNet2-B6

Yakovlev & Okhotnikov · 2026

ReDimNet2 switches between treating the spectrogram as an image (2D) and as a sequence (1D) by reshaping the same features, with pooling over time in the 1D path. That makes a small model very good at telling voices apart. MT2KD distils its 192-number speaker embedding.

Parameters12.3M
ArchitectureReDimNet2 (2D/1D reshaping)
Output192-D speaker embedding
Checkpointtrained on VoxCeleb2 only

Why ReDimNet2: replaces ECAPA-TDNN from the paper. We measured 0.408% EER on VoxCeleb1-O. WavLM was also tried as the speaker teacher and rejected (11.16% EER).

Teaching assistantFrozen · 64M

Zipformer-M

Our first MT2KD student, trained with the three teachers above. The app now runs a smaller 22M Zipformer-S. The gap between 316M-parameter teachers and a 22M student is large, so the finished Zipformer-M sits in between as a teaching assistant (Mirzadeh et al., 2020): a model of the same design, only bigger, whose knowledge is easier for the small student to copy.

  • Start: three of the student’s six layer stacks (S1, S2, S6) are copied from Zipformer-M.
  • Stage 1: besides the three teachers, the student matches Zipformer-M’s encoder features.
  • Stage 2: besides the labels, the student matches Zipformer-M’s transcription, tagging and speaker outputs.
